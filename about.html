Welcome to the Mountain Kingdom

A Country Above the Clouds

Lesotho is a mountainous country in Southern Africa. It is completely surrounded by South Africa and is known for its highlands, beautiful scenery and rich cultural heritage.

Visitors can discover traditional Basotho culture, explore mountain landscapes, enjoy outdoor activities and learn about the country's history and way of life.

Discover Lesotho introduces travellers to some of the country's attractions and experiences while encouraging respectful and responsible tourism.

Frequently Asked Questions

Select a question to read the answer.

Where is Lesotho located?

Lesotho is in Southern Africa and is completely surrounded by the Republic of South Africa.

What is Lesotho famous for?

Lesotho is known for its mountain scenery, high-altitude landscapes, Basotho culture and outdoor tourism experiences.

What languages are spoken in Lesotho?

Sesotho and English are the country's official languages. Sesotho is widely spoken in everyday life.

What can visitors do in Lesotho?

Depending on the location and season, visitors can enjoy hiking, sightseeing, skiing, cultural visits, horse riding and other outdoor activities.
