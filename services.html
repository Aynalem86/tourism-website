Places to Visit

Popular Destinations

Discover some of Lesotho's natural and cultural attractions.

Nature

Maletsunyane Falls

Located near Semonkong, this impressive waterfall is one of Lesotho's best-known natural attractions.

Experience: Waterfall sightseeing, hiking and guided pony trekking in the area.

Ask About Visiting
Scenery

Katse Dam

Katse Dam is a major engineering landmark surrounded by the dramatic mountain scenery of the Lesotho Highlands.

Experience: Scenic viewpoints, dam tours and selected water-based activities.

Ask About Visiting
Nature

Sehlabathebe National Park

Located in the Qacha's Nek district, this national park is known for its distinctive landscape, wildlife, rock formations and natural scenery.

Experience: Nature walks, birdwatching and sightseeing.

Ask About Visiting
Adventure

Afriski Mountain Resort

Located in the Maluti Mountains, Afriski is known for its winter snow-sport experiences.

Experience: Seasonal snow activities and mountain scenery.

Ask About Visiting
Culture

Thaba-Bosiu

Discover an important place in Basotho history and learn about the heritage of the nation.

Experience: Historical sightseeing and cultural learning.

Ask About Visiting
Nature

Bokong Nature Reserve

Explore high-altitude scenery and appreciate the natural environment of the Lesotho Highlands.

Experience: Scenic drives, nature appreciation and birdwatching.
